How to Play Turbo Car Racer 🏎️

🎮 Steering: Tap the ◀ and ▶ on-screen buttons, or drag your finger anywhere on screen to smoothly glide through traffic lanes.

🚀 Nitro Turbo: Hold Nitro (or Spacebar / ▲ Up) to ignite blue exhaust fire and double your speed!

🛑 Brake / Slow Down: Tap Brake (or ▼ Down) to avoid high-speed pileups.

🔥 Close Overtake Bonus: Passing traffic vehicles within inches grants a "CLOSE CALL! +150 PTS" bonus multiplier!

🤖 AI Auto-Drive: Tap AI Auto-Drive in the bottom bar to let algorithmic intelligence navigate heavy highway traffic automatically.
