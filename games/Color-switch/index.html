How to Play 🔄

👆 Tap anywhere on the screen (or press Space / ↑) to make the ball hop upwards.

🎨 Color Match Rule: You can only pass through segments matching your ball's current color! Touching other colors costs 1 life.

🔮 Color Switcher Orbs: Passing through a multi-color switcher orb transforms your ball into a new color!

⭐ Stars: Collect golden stars inside obstacles to boost your score.
