How to Play 🦘

🕹️ Movement: Use ← / → buttons, Arrow keys, A/D, or touch/drag on the left/right screen.

🎯 Auto-Bounce: Landing on platforms from above bounces your Doodler upwards automatically.

🚀 Power-Ups: Grab Springs, Propeller Caps, Jetpacks, and Bubble Shields for huge altitude boosts!
