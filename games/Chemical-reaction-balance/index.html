How to Balance Chemical Equations 🧪

🔬 Law of Conservation of Mass: Matter cannot be created or destroyed. The number of atoms for each element on the Reactants side (Left) must exactly equal the Products side (Right).

🔢 Coefficients vs Subscripts: You can only change the big coefficients in front of compounds (e.g. 2 H₂O = 4 Hydrogen, 2 Oxygen). Subscripts within formulas are fixed molecules.

📱 Touch Steppers: Tap the + and − buttons beside each molecule to increase or decrease coefficients effortlessly without keyboard popups.
