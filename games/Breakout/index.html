How to Play Breakout 🧱

🏓 Paddle Control: Drag your finger anywhere across the screen to slide your paddle smoothly left & right.

🎯 Angular Bounce: Hitting the ball near the edges of the paddle angles it sharply outward for targeted strikes.

💥 Special Bricks: Red TNT blocks detonate adjacent bricks; armored bricks take 2 hits to shatter!

🎁 Power-Up Drops: Catch falling capsules for Multi-Ball, Laser Blasters, Wide Paddle, and Fireball Penetration.

🤖 AI Auto-Pilot: Turn on Auto-Pilot for algorithmic trajectory defense.
