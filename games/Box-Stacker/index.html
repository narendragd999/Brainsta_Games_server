How to Play 📦

🎯 Objective: Move the entire stack of numbered boxes from the Left Pillar to the Right Pillar.

📦 Movement: Tap a pillar to pick up its top box, then tap another pillar to place it down (or drag & drop!).

⚠️ Golden Rule: You can only move one box at a time, and a larger box can never be placed on top of a smaller box.

⚡ Auto-Solve & Hint: Tap Next Move Hint for the optimal step, or Auto Solve to watch the recursive step-by-step solver in action!
